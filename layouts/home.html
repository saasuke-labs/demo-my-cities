The notebook

Pick a city

Each page has what to see, what to eat, how to get in from the airport, this week’s forecast and the best months to go.

  • 01BCN Spain Barcelona Gaudí’s curves, a grid you can read from the air, and dinner at ten.
  • 02TYO Japan Tokyo Neon and temple bells, trains to the second, and ramen at midnight.
  • 03NYC United States New York Five boroughs, one grid, and a skyline that is never finished.
  • 04ROM Italy Rome Two thousand years stacked on top of each other, and espresso standing up.
  • 05SYD Australia Sydney Harbour ferries, sandstone headlands and a swim before work.
  • 06PAR France Paris Zinc rooftops, river light and the long lunch.

Under the hood

Three small tools, one site

Nothing here runs in your browser except a few lines of script. The pages are generated once, in CI, and served as files.

Site generator

Gengo

Turns a YAML manifest, Markdown files and HTML templates into this site. It also fetches each city’s weather while it builds. 

$ gengo generate --output dist

Diagrams as code

Nagare

Draws every chart and diagram from a few lines of text inside the Markdown. No image files, no charting library in the page. 

airport.e --> train.w

Comments and reactions

Kotomi

Gives static pages a voice: readers react and comment, you moderate, and the words stay on your own server. 

new Kotomi({ pageId: "tokyo" })
